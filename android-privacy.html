PixPalette: Paint by Numbers

Privacy Policy for Android

Effective November 25, 2026

PixPalette for Android has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your pictures and paintings stay on your device and are never sent to us. (Using an iPhone or iPad? See the iPhone and iPad privacy policy.)

What stays on your device

  • Your pictures and paintings. Photos you take or choose, the puzzles made from them, your progress and your finished paintings are stored only in the app on your device. We never see them.
  • Camera and photos. To take a picture, PixPalette opens your phone's own camera app, and to choose one it uses Android's photo picker. The app only receives the one picture you take or pick, and doesn't need permission to see your other photos.
  • Saving a finished painting adds that one image to your phone's Pictures folder.
  • Printing goes from your device to your printer using Android's own printing system.

Picture search

When you search for pictures, your search words are sent to Openverse, a free library of openly licensed images, and the pictures you see are loaded from the websites that host them (such as Wikimedia Commons and Flickr). Those services receive your search and your device's internet address, as any website does. We don't receive your searches. The ten pictures built into the app need no internet at all.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ins, names or email addresses.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking, and no sharing with data brokers.
  • We have no server for this app and never sell or rent information.

Children

PixPalette is made to be enjoyed by all ages and contains no ads or purchases. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your device (paintings you saved to Pictures stay there).
  • If you don't use picture search, the app never connects to the internet.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's update notes.
