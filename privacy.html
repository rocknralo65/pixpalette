PixPalette: Paint by Numbers

Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

PixPalette has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your pictures and paintings stay on your device. The only time a picture leaves your device is when you choose an AI style, and then only to make that one picture.

What stays on your device

  • Your pictures and paintings. Photos you take or choose, the puzzles made from them, your progress and your finished paintings are stored only in the app on your device. We never see them.
  • Camera and Photos. The app uses the camera or opens your photos only when you tap the button to add a picture, and only reads the picture you choose. Saving a finished painting to Photos adds that one image. You can change these permissions any time in Settings.
  • Printing goes straight from your device to your printer using Apple's printing system.

When you use an AI style

AI styles (cartoon, oil painting and watercolor) are made on a server, not on your device. When you tap one:

  • A copy of that one picture, reduced to about 1,000 pixels across, is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and then to Replicate, the service that runs the AI model. The styled picture comes back to your device.
  • We do not keep your picture or the result on our server. Replicate processes it under its own privacy policy and, for this kind of request, deletes it automatically after a short time.
  • Please don't use AI styles on pictures you don't have the right to use.

Free pictures and the subscription

  • Free AI pictures. To count the free AI pictures on each device, the app uses Apple's DeviceCheck. Apple keeps a tiny counter for your device; we receive only an anonymous token that lets us read and update it. It does not identify you.
  • Subscription. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app sends us an anonymous purchase number, which we check with Apple to confirm the subscription is active.
  • Monthly count. Our server keeps a count of AI pictures made under each subscription each month, stored against that anonymous purchase number. Counts are deleted automatically after about 40 days.

Picture search

When you search for pictures, your search words are sent to Openverse, a free library of openly licensed images, and the pictures you see are loaded from the websites that host them (such as Wikimedia Commons and Flickr). Those services receive your search and your device's internet address, as any website does. The ten pictures built into the app need no internet at all.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ins, names or email addresses.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking, and no sharing with data brokers.
  • We never sell or rent information.

Our server

Our server runs on Cloudflare. Like any website host, Cloudflare may briefly record technical details such as internet addresses to keep the service secure. We don't use them to identify anyone.

Children

PixPalette is made to be enjoyed by all ages and contains no ads or links that lead out of the app. Buying the subscription happens through Apple, so families can use Apple's Ask to Buy and Screen Time controls. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your device.
  • You can turn off camera and Photos access in Settings.
  • Not using AI styles means no picture ever leaves your device.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's update notes.
